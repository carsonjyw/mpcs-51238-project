All your money, on one desk.

Ledgerline lays your bank and brokerage accounts out side by side, the way you'd spread papers on a desk. Its AI leaves sticky notes, tied with string to the things they explain, so you can see how one account affects another.

Drag to move around · click a sticky note to see what it ties together
Related things sit together

The paycheck sits beside checking, checking beside savings, and the two brokerage folders side by side, so connections are easy to see.

Notes tie it all together

Each AI sticky note is pinned between the accounts it's about, with string running to each one. Click a note to light them up.

Your desk, your accounts

Untick an account in the tray and it comes off the desk, along with any notes about it.

Spread out the full demo desk

A bigger desk, plus “ask the desk” to find any account or note. Everything is mock data.
