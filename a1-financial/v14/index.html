What happens to your money if…?

Ledgerline brings your bank and brokerage accounts together, then lets you try simple what-ifs. Flip a switch or move a slider, and see your checking, savings and investments change together, with a plain-English explanation of why.

Start from today

Every what-if begins with your real balances (mock here), so the numbers are yours, not a generic example.

All your accounts react together

Moving money to savings, a bad week for stocks, a paycheck landing: see checking, savings and investments respond at once.

Illustrations, not advice

The lab shows what the numbers would look like. It doesn't predict markets or tell you what to do.

Try your own what-ifs

In the demo you can also type a question like “what if stocks fall 15%?” Everything is mock data.
