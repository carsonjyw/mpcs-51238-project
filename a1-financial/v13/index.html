Ledgerline lines up your bank and brokerage accounts in one document and compares them with last month, or with the last time you checked. Unchanged lines fold away, changed lines show the difference, and the AI comments on the ones that matter.

One document, every account

Cash, spending and investments line up in one place, so a change in one sits right above the change it caused in another.

Only what moved

Lines that didn't change fold away. Open them any time, but they stay out of your way.

Comments where they matter

The AI leaves short notes on the lines worth explaining, the way a reviewer comments on a document.

Review the full demo diff

Switch comparisons, filter accounts, jump between changes and search for any line. Everything is mock data.
