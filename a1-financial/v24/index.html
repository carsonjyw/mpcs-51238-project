Bank + brokerage accounts, explained

See all your money in one place, and understand what changed.

Ledgerline brings checking, savings, cards and brokerage accounts like Schwab and IBKR into one view. Its AI explains every change in plain language, so you know where you stand without switching between apps.

MOCK DATA Bank balances as of Mon, Oct 5, 2026 · stock prices as of Fri, Oct 2 close

Any dates you choose

Pick a preset, drag across the chart or type exact dates. The chart, the breakdown and the assistant all use the same period.

Why it changed

Every change is split into income, spending and investment gains. Moving money between your own accounts never counts as a gain.

Every transaction, searchable

Search and filter bank and card transactions by date, merchant, category, account or amount. Click one to see how it changed your balance.
