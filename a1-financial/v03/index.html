What goes on the screen

Bank, brokerage, or both: your call.

Each account type adds its own panels. The AI works across whatever you include.

Bank accounts

Where your cash goes

  • Balances for checking, savings and cards
  • Income from paychecks and deposits
  • Spending by category vs. your average
  • Cash flow: in, out, and kept each month
Brokerage accounts

What your portfolio did

  • Holdings from Schwab, IBKR and more in one table
  • Daily gains & losses on a calendar
  • Per-position moves: day %, day $ and value
  • Portfolio changes month by month
AI

Why it changed

  • Explains moves in plain language with the numbers
  • Summarizes any day or month
  • Answers questions across the accounts you picked
  • Never gives personal financial advice
