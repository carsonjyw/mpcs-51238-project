What Ledgerline reads, and what it tells you

Pick bank, brokerage, or both. Each one adds its own panels to the terminal, and the AI analyst works across everything you connect.

Bank accounts

Where your cash goes

  • Balances across checking, savings and cards
  • Income detected from deposits and paychecks
  • Spending by category, compared with your average
  • Cash flow: month-by-month income minus spending
Brokerage accounts

What your portfolio did

  • Holdings & positions across Schwab, IBKR and others
  • Daily gains & losses, per position and in total
  • Portfolio changes: allocation, concentration, overlap
  • One table, even when the same ticker sits in two accounts
AI analyst

Why it changed

  • Explains moves in plain language, with the numbers
  • Summarizes your day, week or month across accounts
  • Answers questions like “Why is checking down?”
  • Points to the data behind every note
