How Ledgerline decides what goes where

Every morning it compares your accounts with your own usual patterns, then files each change in one of three groups.

Worth a look

Something unusual or with a deadline: spending well above your average, a bill coming due, a portfolio leaning heavily one way.

Good to know

Real changes that need no action: a paycheck landing, a month's investment result, a day the market moved.

All clear

Things that went as expected, kept out of your way so the important items stand out.

What it reads

Connect bank accounts, brokerage accounts, or both. Untick an account above and its items leave the inbox.

Bank accounts
  • Balances for checking, savings and cards
  • Income from paychecks and deposits
  • Spending by category vs. your average
  • Cash flow: in, out and kept each month
Brokerage accounts
  • Holdings across Schwab, IBKR and more
  • Daily gains & losses, shown on a calendar
  • Portfolio changes: allocation and concentration
  • The AI explains each move and answers questions
